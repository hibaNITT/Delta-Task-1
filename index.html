RULES OF THE GAME

Read these carefully before playing =_=

ENTROPY is a strategic turn-based game played on a 6*12 grid. 1.Each cell has a capacity equal to its number of orthogonal neighbors: 2 for corners, 3 for edge cells, and 4 for interior cells. On your turn, you must add one unit to a cell: 2.Your very first move can be placed in any empty cell (starting with capacity - 1 units). After that, you can only place on cells that already contain your own units. 3.After placing, if any cell reaches its capacity, it explodes: the cell is cleared, and one unit is sent to each orthogonal neighbor. This can trigger a chain reaction of further explosions. Any units (yours or your opponent’s) that are present in cells affected by the explosions are captured and converted to the player who triggered the explosion.So that player becomes its owner 4.The game is played with the players taking next to next turns. Each player has a turn timer of 10 seconds and there is a shared game timer of 10 minutes. NOTE- If a player does not complete his move within 10 sec - 5 points will be deducted from his score. If his score is less that 5 he will get a 0. 5.The game ends when: All units on the board belong to a single player, or The overall game timer expires then the highest score wins. 6.A player's score is the sum of the number of opponent units captured and the length of chain reactions triggered. 7.Teleportation - Some of the cells are coloured(do not consider the green cell). These colored cells are in pairs. If an explosion occurs in that cell then that explosion is transfered to the location where the cell of the same color is placed(basically the two cells of the same color are partners) NOTE - This can be very helpful when you want to escape that place. 8. This game has 2 cool POWER UPS- - Row Ripper : This power up moves along a vertical path outside the board , if you manage to cause an explosion exactly on the row where the row ripper is present then all the cells in that row becomes yours. If the cell was - empty : then one of your piece will be placed on that cell - owned by you : one of ur piece is added - owned by another player : his pieces will vanish and one of ur piece is added - Fortress Cell : once you take ownership of the neon green colour cells you will be safe. If the opponent tries to capture the contents on this cell, this feature will protect you. You will lose ownership only if your pieces explode and move out then the next player is free to capture this cell 9. This Game also has two evil SABOTAGES - - Lockdown: your move should stimulate exactly 3 explosions during the chain reaction not more not less. Then for the next 3 turns, your opponent cannot trigger their explosions. They can only place pieces in cells that are below maximum capacity. -Chaos Drift : your move should stimulate exactly 5 explosions during the chain reaction not more not less This Randomizes the opponent’s next move —instead of choosing freely, their piece lands in a random valid cell. Plan your moves carefully — one well-placed unit can trigger a massive chain reaction that reshapes the entire board! Good luck, and may your reactions be explosive!

    Power Ups & Sabotages

    Quick reference for the icons and special effects used on the board.

    Row Ripper

    Triggers a row-wide sweep when an explosion lands on the moving indicator row.

    Fortress Cell

    A protected neon-green cell that blocks enemy capture until your pieces leave it.

    Lockdown

    Forces a target player to avoid explosion-triggering moves for the next three turns. And if the remaining moves of the player include only explosion moves you win!!!

    Chaos Drift

    Randomizes the target player’s next placement so they lose direct control of the move.
